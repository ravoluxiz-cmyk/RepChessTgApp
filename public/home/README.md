# Home page assets

Photographs and logo were exported from the Rep Chess brandbook archive supplied by the site owner on 2026-10-13. They illustrate the wider Rep Chess community; published Krasnodar gallery photographs take precedence on the page.

- `rep-chess-logo.png`: original logo from brandbook page 8, with its proportions preserved.
- `chess-evening.jpg`, `chess-friends.jpg`: original photos embedded in brandbook page 22.
- `chess-community.jpg`: original photo embedded in brandbook page 3.
- `chess-park.jpg`, `chess-cafe.jpg`: two other original photos embedded in brandbook page 22. Five distinct photos are allocated across the hero and gallery, with no reuse.
- `rep-chess-krd-white.svg`, `rep-chess-krd-white.png`: original Krasnodar city-logo vector outlines from brandbook page 11, exported in white on transparent background without the original city-label rectangle. Letter shapes and relative positions are preserved.
- `rep-chess-krd-logo-white.svg`, `rep-chess-krd-logo-white.png`: corrected export used on the site. Compound contours preserve transparent R/P/D counters; the three lines share a left edge. The filename changed to prevent old image-cache responses during release.
- `app-home.png`: official publicly published screen at https://repchess.ru/app/app-screenshot.png.
- `app-rating.png`, `app-clock.png`: official app promotional screenshots from https://www.rustore.ru/catalog/app/ru.nfg.repchess. CSS shows the phone screen within each existing promotion image; the source files are unchanged.

The personal profile screenshot supplied in the conversation is not included.

The homepage fonts in `public/fonts/benzin-brand-*.ttf` are the original Benzin Regular and Benzin Extra Bold from the public font-materials ZIP linked on brandbook page 26. They are scoped to the new homepage; typography on other pages is unchanged.
